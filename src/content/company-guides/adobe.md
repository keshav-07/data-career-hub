## Preparation overview

Prepare in three areas: technical fundamentals, data system design, and behavioural stories. Subscription businesses need correct history (plans change over time) and reconciliation with billing, which shape the questions to expect.

## Technology focus

Revise slowly changing dimensions, reconciliation and SQL: [SCD Type 2](/data-warehousing/slowly-changing-dimensions/), [data quality checks](/interview/data-engineering/data-quality-checks/), [SQL fundamentals](/sql/sql-fundamentals/).

## System-design focus

Practise the [cloud data warehouse](/data-engineering/system-design/cloud-data-warehouse-platform/) and [reporting platform](/data-engineering/system-design/reporting-analytics-platform/) case studies.

## Behavioural preparation

Prepare examples of impact you can describe honestly and of communicating clearly with stakeholders.
