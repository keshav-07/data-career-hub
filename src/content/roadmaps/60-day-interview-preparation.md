This plan assumes you already know the fundamentals (see the [30-day fundamentals plan](/roadmaps/30-day-data-engineering-fundamentals/) if not) and want to convert them into interview performance.

## Rules for the 60 days

- **Practise out loud.** Interviews test explanation as much as knowledge.
- **Time yourself.** Speed comes from repetition, not from reading more.
- **Keep an error log.** Write down every mistake and revisit it at the end of each week.
- **Protect rest days.** Consistency over eight weeks beats bursts.
