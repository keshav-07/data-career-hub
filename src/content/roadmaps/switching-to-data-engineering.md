Analysts and developers are among the most common routes into Data Engineering, and both already have half of what the role needs. The work is identifying the other half and proving you have closed it.

## The honest picture

- Titles vary: "analytics engineer", "data platform engineer" and "data engineer" overlap. Read responsibilities, not titles.
- Real data-engineering work in your current job is the strongest evidence you can show; look for it first.
- A transition story should be specific: what you built, what you learned, why the new role fits.
