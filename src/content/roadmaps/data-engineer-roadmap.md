This roadmap is a **learning map, not a checklist of everything**. Work through the stages in order; each one builds on the last. Time estimates assume roughly 8 to 12 focused hours a week and vary a lot with prior experience, so treat them as guidance, not targets.

Skip a stage only if you can already meet its outcome. Spend your time on the outcomes, not on finishing pages.
