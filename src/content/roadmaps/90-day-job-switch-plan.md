Working engineers have less time but more experience to draw on. This plan spends that time where it matters: the gaps your target roles actually care about, one convincing project, and focused applications.

## Principles

- Start from **real job descriptions**, not generic lists.
- Your current job is evidence: mine it for stories, scale and trade-offs you handled.
- Apply while preparing in the final weeks. Waiting until you feel "ready" usually means waiting too long.
